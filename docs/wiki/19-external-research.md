# External Research — Industry Patterns

Research checked on 2026-08-30. These are **design inputs**, not business requirements for Africa Aluminium.

## ReSpark

ReSpark emphasizes connecting purchase/sales orders to tickets, invoices and shipments, tracking fulfilment, and eliminating re-entry. Its current product messaging also emphasizes inventory, pricing, dispatch, finance and role-based permissions in one system.

Source:
- https://respark.com/features/purchasing-sales-orders
- https://respark.com/
- https://respark.com/features/brokerage-trading
- https://respark.com/features/supplier-portal

Useful pattern for this project:

> Terms should travel with the material from the original deal through operational execution and finance.

## Scraplytics

Scraplytics emphasizes real-time inventory, supplier history, dispatch, invoicing, payments and analytics. It also highlights weighted-average cost and supplier self-service capabilities.

Source:
- https://scraplytics.com/
- https://scraplytics.com/features
- https://scraplytics.com/features/supplier-portal

Useful pattern for this project:

> Material quantity, supplier transactions, inventory, costing and invoicing belong to one connected operational data model.

## AMCS / RECY

AMCS markets recycling-specific enterprise software covering yard/industrial operations, inventory, business processes and connected management functionality.

Source:
- https://www.amcsgroup.com/solutions/enterprise-management/recy-systems/

Useful pattern for this project:

> Recycling software benefits from industry-specific workflows instead of generic ERP screens.

## Research conclusion

Across the researched products, recurring patterns are:

1. Real-time operational data.
2. Connected contracts/orders and transactions.
3. Inventory linked to inbound/outbound events.
4. Supplier/customer history.
5. Financial/operational reporting.
6. Mobile/field workflow support.
7. Automation and AI layered on top of structured transactional data.

The project's opportunity is to adapt these patterns to the simpler, field-heavy, African recycling procurement context rather than importing enterprise complexity wholesale.
