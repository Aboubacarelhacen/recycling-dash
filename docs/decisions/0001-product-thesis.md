# ADR 0001 — Product Thesis

## Decision

Treat the system as a **contract-driven recycling operations platform**, not an Excel replacement or generic accounting app.

## Why

The company's real workflow starts with client commitments and ends with fulfilment and invoicing. Procurement and operating costs are the mechanism by which margin is created or destroyed.

## Consequence

The contract becomes a first-class domain object and the main management workspace.
