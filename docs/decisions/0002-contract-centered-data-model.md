# ADR 0002 — Contract-Centered Data Model

## Decision

Whenever operationally appropriate, purchases, expenses, deliveries and invoices should be linkable to the contract they support.

## Why

This makes contract fulfillment and profitability explainable.

## Caveat

Not every cost will always be contract-attributable. The system must support company-level/unallocated costs rather than inventing allocations.
