# Premium Landing Page Blueprint

## Positioning

### Hero

**Run every recycling contract with precision.**

Subheadline:

> From client commitments to field procurement, inventory, expenses and final delivery — every kilogram and every cost connected in one operating platform.

Primary CTA: `Book a Demo`
Secondary CTA: `Explore Platform`

## Narrative

### 1. The problem

“Your operation should not live across spreadsheets, paper receipts and scattered messages.”

### 2. The system

```text
Contract → Procurement → Inventory → Delivery → Invoice → Profit
```

### 3. The cockpit

Use the real dashboard UI as the visual centerpiece.

### 4. Procurement

Show the field purchase flow and supplier history.

### 5. Margin

Show how the platform moves from raw spend to real contract economics.

### 6. Field operations

Show mobile/offline workflow.

### 7. Documents

Show one-click invoice/receipt generation.

### 8. Intelligence

Show contract health and exception detection.

### Final CTA

> **Your operation is already running. It's time your software caught up.**

## Visual direction

Premium industrial technology. Avoid generic “eco” design tropes.

Use:

- graphite
- green accent
- white content surfaces
- refined typography
- product screenshots
- subtle motion
- high contrast
- excellent whitespace

## Hero animation

A strong option is a slow camera/zoom into the dashboard rather than a decorative recycling animation. The message should be “operational visibility,” not “environmental marketing.”
